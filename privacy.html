Website Privacy Policy

Operated by Bramora Labs  ·  Last updated: August 2026

This policy covers this website — the marketing pages you're reading right now. It is separate from the Budget Tracker Android app's privacy policy, which covers your financial data inside the app and is far stricter, since the app collects nothing at all. See the app's Privacy Policy for that.

1. The short version

This website does not use analytics, advertising, or tracking scripts, and does not set cookies. The only personal data it collects is your email address, and only if you voluntarily submit it through the "Get notified at launch" signup — nothing is collected just by browsing the site.

2. What happens if you sign up for launch updates

If you submit your email address, it's sent to and stored by Mailchimp (The Rocket Science Group, LLC), an email service provider we use to send you: a one-time notification when Budget Tracker launches on Google Play, and occasional product updates or promotional emails about Budget Tracker afterward if you don't unsubscribe. We do not sell, rent, or share your email with anyone else for their own marketing.

Mailchimp acts as our data processor for this — it stores and sends on our instructions, under its own privacy policy and data processing terms. Every email we send through it includes an unsubscribe link, which is enforced by Mailchimp's platform and works immediately.

To unsubscribe or delete your email from our list, use the unsubscribe link in any email we send, or email bramoralabsupport@gmail.com and we'll remove it within 30 days.

3. Cookies and tracking

This site sets no cookies of its own and includes no third-party analytics, advertising, or tracking scripts — nothing from Google Analytics, Meta Pixel, or similar. The small script that runs the scroll-fade animation on this site only reads the page's own layout; it doesn't collect or transmit anything.

4. Hosting

This site is hosted on GitHub Pages (GitHub, Inc. / Microsoft). Like most web hosts, GitHub logs visitor IP addresses at the infrastructure level for security and legal-compliance purposes — this is standard server access logging, not something Bramora Labs requests, collects, or has access to. See GitHub Pages' own data collection notice for details.

5. Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have rights under GDPR, UK GDPR, CCPA, the UAE's PDPL, or similar laws — including the right to know what we hold about you, correct it, delete it, or object to its use. Since the only personal data we hold is an email address you gave us voluntarily, exercising any of these is as simple as emailing bramoralabsupport@gmail.com or clicking unsubscribe.

6. Children's privacy

This site and its signup form are not directed at children, and we don't knowingly collect data from anyone under 16.

7. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy if what the site collects changes — for example, if we later add real analytics, we'll disclose it here first.

8. Governing law

This policy is governed by the laws of the United Arab Emirates, without regard to conflict-of-law principles.

9. Contact

Questions about this policy: bramoralabsupport@gmail.com
