Website Privacy Policy

Operated by Bramora Labs  ·  Last updated: August 2026

This policy covers this website — the marketing pages you're reading right now. It is separate from the Budget Tracker Android app's privacy policy, which covers your financial data inside the app and is far stricter, since the app collects nothing at all. See the app's Privacy Policy for that. This document was written to describe our practices honestly and in plain language, but it isn't a substitute for advice from a qualified lawyer — if you need a legally certain answer, especially about a specific jurisdiction, ask one.

1. The short version

This website does not use analytics, advertising, or tracking scripts, and does not set cookies. Just browsing this site doesn't send us anything. The only way we receive any personal information right now is if you choose to email us — see Section 2 for exactly how that currently works, and Section 3 for a planned change to that.

2. "Get notified at launch" — how it works right now

The "Get notified at launch" button is currently a plain mailto: link. Clicking it opens your own email app with a message pre-addressed to us and the subject line already filled in — nothing is sent automatically, and we receive nothing unless you personally press send in your own email app. If you do send it, that email lands in our normal support inbox (bramoralabsupport@gmail.com), the same place all our support email goes, and is read by the same person who builds the app. We don't have an automated system to email you back today — sending it just lets us know you're interested.

3. Planned: a real email signup (not live yet)

This section describes a feature we intend to add, not something currently running on this site. We're documenting it here in advance so it's covered by this policy the moment it goes live, and so you know what to expect if you see it.

We plan to replace the mailto link above with a real signup form connected to Mailchimp (The Rocket Science Group, LLC), an email service provider. Once that's live, submitting your email there will mean: Mailchimp stores it on our behalf and sends you a one-time notification when Budget Tracker launches on Google Play, plus occasional product updates about Budget Tracker afterward, until you unsubscribe. We will not sell, rent, or share your email with anyone else for their own marketing.

Mailchimp would act as our data processor for this — storing and sending only on our instructions, under its own privacy policy and data processing terms. Every marketing email sent through it is required to include a working unsubscribe link, enforced by Mailchimp's platform. We'll update this section — including retention details — to match exactly how it's configured once it's actually connected, and remove this "not live yet" notice at that point.

To unsubscribe or ask us to delete an email you've sent us, email bramoralabsupport@gmail.com. Once the Mailchimp signup is live, every email it sends will also include a self-service unsubscribe link.

4. Cookies and tracking

This site sets no cookies of its own and includes no third-party analytics, advertising, or tracking scripts — nothing from Google Analytics, Meta Pixel, or similar. The small script that runs the scroll-fade animation on this site only reads the page's own layout; it doesn't collect or transmit anything.

5. Hosting

This site is hosted on GitHub Pages (GitHub, Inc. / Microsoft). Like most web hosts, GitHub logs visitor IP addresses at the infrastructure level for security and legal-compliance purposes — this is standard server access logging, not something Bramora Labs requests, collects, or has access to. See GitHub Pages' own data collection notice for details.

6. Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have rights under laws like the GDPR, UK GDPR, CCPA, or the UAE's PDPL over personal data a company holds about you — for example, to know what's held, correct it, delete it, or object to its use. Right now, the only personal information we hold about you is whatever you've chosen to email us, exactly like any other email you send someone. Reaching out about any of this is as simple as emailing bramoralabsupport@gmail.com. We haven't determined which of these specific rights formally apply to a business our size and haven't had this reviewed by a lawyer — if that matters to you, ask us and we'll do our best to help regardless.

7. Children's privacy

This site is not directed at children, and we don't knowingly collect data from anyone under 16.

8. Changes to this policy

We'll update this policy whenever what the site actually does changes — most immediately, when the Mailchimp signup in Section 3 goes live, and again if we ever add analytics or anything else.

9. Governing law

This policy is intended to be governed by the laws of the United Arab Emirates, where Bramora Labs is based. This hasn't been reviewed by a lawyer, and if you're relying on this for a specific legal question, get independent advice.

10. Contact

Questions about this policy: bramoralabsupport@gmail.com
