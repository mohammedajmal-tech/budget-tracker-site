FAQ

Frequently asked

Is Budget Tracker free?

Yes. Every core feature — accounts, transactions, budgets, the Zakat calculator, remittance tracking, multi-currency support — is free. A single one-time purchase unlocks CSV/Excel export and PDF reports.

Does it work offline?

Yes. Everything is stored in an encrypted database on your device. No internet connection or bank linking required, and your financial data never leaves your phone unless you export it.

Does it have ads?

No ads anywhere, and no ad SDK in the build.
