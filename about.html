About

Built by one person, for a problem he had himself

Bramora Labs — UAE

Budget Tracker is made by Bramora Labs, an independent, one-person software studio based in the UAE. There's no investor deck behind it and no growth team — just one developer who moved to the UAE, tried the popular budgeting apps, and found that none of them were built with expat life in mind.

The gaps were specific: apps that assumed one currency, not a salary in AED and a family back home in a different one. Apps with no way to calculate Zakat without switching to a separate tool. Apps that wanted bank credentials linked in before they'd show you anything. Budget Tracker exists to close those gaps, without asking for data it doesn't need.

What that means in practice

  • No bank linking. The app never asks for banking credentials or connects to any financial institution.
  • No ads, no ad SDK. Nothing in the codebase tracks you for advertising, because there's no advertising.
  • Encrypted, on-device storage. Your data lives in a SQLCipher-encrypted local database. It doesn't touch a server unless you explicitly export or back it up yourself.
  • One-time payment, not a subscription. The optional Pro unlock (export and PDF reports) is a single Google Play purchase, not a recurring charge.
